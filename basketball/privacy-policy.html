Privacy Policy

Basketball Star · superRGB

Last updated 18 November 2026

superRGB ("we", "us" or "our") develops Basketball Star. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Android game and contact us for support. The game is intended for players aged 13 and older.

For privacy questions and data requests, email draymond2018@gmail.com.

Information handled by the game

  • Information stored on your device. The game stores music and sound settings and game-related records, which may include tutorial status, team and player selections, tournament state, scores, achievements and recent play time. These records support gameplay and preferences. Android may include local app data in system backups according to your device and backup settings.
  • Advertising information. Google AdMob may process your IP address and an approximate location inferred from it, Android advertising ID, app set ID and other relevant device or account identifiers, ad impressions, taps and video interactions, and app or SDK performance and diagnostic information. The information available depends on device settings, consent status and service configuration. Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.
  • Usage analytics. The game's analytics component starts when the app opens. It sends an Android ID or a generated device identifier, application identifier and version, device type, operating system and version, and event types and timestamps to superRGB's reporting service at game.kungapp.com. Events can include installation, app starts and exits, supported screen or interaction events, and crash events. Our reporting service also receives the connection's IP address.
  • Support information. When you email us, we receive your email address and the content and attachments you choose to send. We use this information to handle your enquiry, troubleshoot a problem or process a privacy request.

How information is used

We use relevant information to operate the game, preserve preferences and game records, understand usage and technical problems, improve stability, deliver and measure advertisements, detect invalid advertising activity, respond to support requests and meet applicable legal obligations.

The game includes interstitial advertisements between a completed match and the results screen. Advertisement content and personalization depend on the advertising provider's configuration, applicable requirements and available privacy choices. Google's consent tools check whether advertising requests can proceed and may display a privacy message. Analytics initialization is a separate process and begins at app startup.

Services that handle information

  • Google AdMob and Google services. Google provides advertising, consent tools and integrated Google Play services. These services may handle identifiers, network information, interactions and diagnostics under Google's applicable terms and privacy practices. Google Play or your device's backup provider may also process information for app distribution, platform services and backups. Google Privacy Policy.
  • superRGB analytics using ThinkingData technology. The app uses a customized ThinkingData component to send the usage information described above to game.kungapp.com, a reporting service operated by superRGB. We process these reports using our service and its hosting infrastructure. ThinkingData supplies the underlying technology, and its SDK statement describes how its tools work. ThinkingData SDK privacy statement.
  • Time synchronization. The analytics component contacts Apple's time.apple.com service to align event timestamps. That service receives the network IP address and time-synchronization requests. Apple Privacy Policy.

Information may also be disclosed where legally required, to respond to lawful requests, or to establish or defend legal claims. External advertisements and websites have their own privacy practices, which apply when you interact with those services.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can manage available advertising ID and advertising privacy controls in your Android device settings. The names and availability of controls vary by Android version and device. Choices made through a Google consent message are handled by Google's consent tools. For further assistance with your privacy preferences, contact us using the email below.

You can clear the game's local records using Android Settings, then Apps, Basketball Star, Storage, and Clear storage or Clear data. This resets locally stored progress and preferences. Backup copies are managed through your device's backup settings, and a restored backup may restore local records.

To request access, correction or deletion of information associated with your use of the game, email draymond2018@gmail.com and mention Basketball Star. Please describe your request and provide information that helps locate the relevant records, such as your device model and approximate dates of use. We may ask for proportionate additional information to verify and fulfil the request.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may also have rights to object to processing, restrict processing, receive a portable copy of your information, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or complain to a data protection authority. We assess requests under applicable law, explain any lawful retention or identification limits, and respond within the applicable time limits. Information handled independently by Google or another provider can also be managed through that provider's privacy tools.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your device until cleared, replaced or removed with the app, subject to Android backup and restoration settings. Analytics, advertising and support records are retained according to their purpose, the need to investigate technical problems or invalid activity, applicable legal obligations, and the relevant service's retention controls.

Our retention approach is to keep personal information only for as long as needed for these purposes, then delete it or render it no longer identifiable. A deletion request is assessed against those purposes and any applicable legal requirements. Clearing device storage affects local records, while deletion of previously transmitted records is handled through a privacy request or the receiving provider's controls.

Data protection and processing locations

Game settings and records use the app's local storage. Advertising and analytics information is transmitted to the services described above. Information protection depends on device security, SDK implementation and the practices of the services that receive information. Google's Mobile Ads documentation describes its use of TLS for advertising data in transit. Google Mobile Ads data handling.

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence. Where cross-border processing is subject to legal requirements, the applicable safeguards and rights must be observed. You can contact us for information about the handling of your data and relevant provider arrangements.

Young players

Basketball Star is intended for players aged 13 and older. Rules on a minor's consent and parental involvement vary by jurisdiction. Where applicable law requires parental or guardian involvement, those requirements apply. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where required.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as the game, its service providers or applicable requirements change. The revised version will show its last-updated date. Where applicable law requires additional notice or consent for a change, those requirements apply.
