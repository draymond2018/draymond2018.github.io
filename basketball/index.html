Where can I get Basketball Star?+

Basketball Star is available for Android on Google Play. Use any Google Play button on this site to open the official listing.

How does 2v2 work?+

On mobile, you play with an AI teammate against two CPU opponents. You can choose your city team and player before the match.

How do special skills work?+

Each player has a signature skill: Fast Break, Mega Dunk or Defence. Wait for the skill to recharge, then use the skill button. Mega Dunk requires possession of the ball.

Does the game include ads?+

Yes. The game includes interstitial ads between completed matches and the results screen.
