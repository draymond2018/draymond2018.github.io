YOUR ACCOUNT & YOUR DATA

Request account deletion

Ready to leave your garden? Contact Ding Ye to request deletion of your Sunlit Garden account and the personal data associated with it.

What will be deleted?

  • Your game account and saved progress.
  • Your inventory, virtual currency and account-linked rewards.
  • Associated profile information and personal data, subject to the exceptions below.

Deletion is permanent once completed. You may lose access to purchased in-game content. Deleting your account does not automatically issue a refund or delete your Google account.

Verification & retained records

We will verify account ownership before processing your request. A player ID alone is not sufficient authorization to delete an account.

Limited transaction, security or dispute records may be retained where required by law or necessary for fraud prevention and resolving claims. We will explain any applicable retention and the expected deletion timeline when responding to your request.

Google manages information it holds independently under its own Privacy Policy.

No game login is required to request help. If you cannot find your player ID or access the game, email us with the information you have. Do not send passwords or complete payment card details.
