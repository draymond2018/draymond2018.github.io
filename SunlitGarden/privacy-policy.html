Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

This policy explains how Ding Ye (the developer of Sunlit Garden, “we”) handles information when you play Sunlit Garden or contact us. Contact: ding2020.ye@gmail.com.

1. Information we process

We process information needed to operate your game account, such as your player ID, profile details, saved progress, inventory, virtual currency, gameplay activity and purchases. If you contact us, we process the contact details and information you provide.

The game and its service providers may process device and app information, IP address, diagnostic information and identifiers needed to provide, secure and troubleshoot the service. Google AdMob may also process advertising identifiers, ad interactions and approximate location inferred from IP address, depending on your device settings, consent and applicable law.

Please do not send passwords, complete payment card details or unnecessary sensitive information when contacting support.

2. How information is used

  • Provide gameplay, save progress and manage your account.
  • Process and verify purchases and deliver in-game content.
  • Show and measure advertisements, including rewarded advertisements.
  • Provide support and process account deletion requests.
  • Maintain security, prevent fraud, diagnose issues and comply with legal obligations.

3. Google Play purchases

Sunlit Garden uses Google Play Billing for in-app purchases. Google processes payment information under its own terms and privacy policy. We may receive purchase records, product identifiers and purchase tokens to verify transactions and deliver purchased content. We do not receive your complete payment card details from Google Play Billing.

Deleting your game account does not automatically refund purchases or cancel a subscription. If you have a subscription, manage or cancel it through Google Play.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

Sunlit Garden uses Google AdMob to display ads. Google and its advertising partners may use device identifiers and other information to deliver, measure and protect advertising services. Ad personalization depends on applicable law, your consent where required and your privacy settings.

You can manage advertising privacy controls in your Android device settings. Where available, you may also change consent choices through the game's privacy options.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Sharing and international processing

Information may be processed by hosting, support, payment and advertising providers to deliver their services. We may disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a lawful business transfer. Google may act as an independent controller for some processing.

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where applicable, appropriate safeguards are required for international transfers.

6. Retention and security

We retain account information as needed to operate the game and fulfill the purposes described above. After a verified deletion request, we delete or anonymize associated personal data except records that must be retained for legal obligations, transaction disputes, security or fraud prevention. Applicable retention details and the expected processing timeline will be explained in our reply.

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

7. Your choices and account deletion

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your personal data, restrict or object to processing, or withdraw consent. Contact us to exercise these rights. We may need to verify account ownership before acting. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

To request deletion of your Sunlit Garden account and associated data, visit our account deletion page or email ding2020.ye@gmail.com. Include your player ID if available. Deletion is permanent and may remove access to game progress and purchased content.

8. Age requirement

Sunlit Garden is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion.

If you are a minor in your country, use the game only with any parental or guardian permission required by applicable law. Local rules may require a higher age for consent to certain data processing.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the game or our practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Where required, we will provide notice of material changes.
